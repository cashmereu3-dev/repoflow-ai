How to Use the Platform

Create Your Organization & Admin Account

Register at repoflowai.online. Your account is set as Administrator by default. Set up your organization name, branding, and subscription tier from the Settings panel.

Invite Your Team

Add lenders, repo managers, and field agents by sending invitation emails. Assign them the appropriate role for their access level. Each user gets a tailored dashboard based on their role.

Add Borrowers & Vehicles

Import or manually enter borrower profiles (name, contact info, SSN last 4, employment). Register vehicles with VIN, make, model, year, color, and lienholder information.

Create a Repossession Assignment

Link a borrower and vehicle to a new assignment. Set the loan balance, priority level, due date, special instructions, and assign a field agent. The system calculates an AI recovery probability score automatically.

Field Agent Works the Case

The assigned agent sees the case in their mobile app. They perform GPS check-ins, upload field photos (AI auto-extracts vehicle data), and update the assignment status as they progress.

Recovery & Close-Out

When a vehicle is recovered, the agent files a recovery report with location, condition, mileage, and impound information. The assignment moves to "Recovered" status. Lender is notified in real time.

Review Analytics & Compliance

Managers and lenders review AI-powered recovery analytics, agent performance dashboards, and the immutable audit log to ensure full compliance and operational visibility.

Security & Compliance

🔐

Row Level Security

PostgreSQL RLS ensures each organization's data is completely isolated. No cross-tenant data leakage is possible at the database level.

📜

Immutable Audit Logs

Every action triggers an immutable database record. Your audit trail can never be altered, deleted, or tampered with.

🔑

JWT Authentication

All sessions are secured with industry-standard JSON Web Tokens via Supabase Auth. Passwords are bcrypt-hashed.

🌐

SSL / HTTPS Everywhere

All traffic is encrypted in transit via TLS. Vercel and Supabase both enforce HTTPS-only connections.

  • Accept your invitation email and create your account

✅ For Borrowers (Self-Service Portal)

  • Receive access link from your lender
  • Log into repoflowai.online/borrower to view your account
